Canary Manufacturing Training Grant

The Canary Manufacturing Training Grant reimburses manufacturers for training new and current employees.

Award

Grants cover 50% of eligible training costs, up to $25,000 per company per year.

Who can apply

  • Manufacturers with a facility in the program area.
  • At least 10 full-time employees.

Deadline

Applications for the next round must be submitted by December 15, 2026.

  • Annual reports
